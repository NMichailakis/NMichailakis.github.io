About Me

Where language
meets intelligence

I'm an undergraduate student of Greek Philology at the National & Kapodistrian University of Athens, specializing in Linguistics. My research focuses on Computational Linguistics — the field where human language and artificial intelligence converge.

I build AI systems that apply real linguistic theory to practical problems. My work bridges theoretical linguistics (syntax, semantics, morphology, pragmatics) with applied AI, creating tools that understand language the way humans do — with nuance, accuracy, and depth.

I hold a C2 Michigan Proficiency in English and a certified Python programming course from the University of Patras. I'm a former award-winning Model UN delegate, having won Best Expert twice.
